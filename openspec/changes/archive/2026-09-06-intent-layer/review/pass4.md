# Pass 4 — dpaa2-verify (`intent-layer`, 648f804..4272019)

## Answers to the mandate's direct questions

**Would one replayer with per-alphabet adapters fold the four ITF modules?** No. The four readers feed four distinct oracles over four distinct pure functions — `itf.rs`→`replay.rs` diffs `reconcile_with` plans per epoch, `intent_itf.rs` asserts `compile` outcome equality, `raw_itf.rs` emits real TOML and matches `parse_str`'s short-circuited error against a refusal set, `edits_itf.rs` re-runs `converge` and the four identity laws per state. A generic replayer would be a framework with exactly one implementation per alphabet (the quint-connect shape the crate already rejected). This mirrors PASS2's "two generations of surface+replay machinery are not duplicates" verdict on the model side. What *is* duplicated is the substrate underneath them — findings F2, F3, F9, F10 below fold that without a framework.

**Do the quint-connect revisit triggers still describe reality?** Half of one fired and was never re-judged — finding F1.

**R11–R13 lints and negative drift tests vs ADR-0013 post-6.x:** structurally sound and live-tied. `tests/ledger_lint.rs:94-149` runs `intent_lint` over the *real* refuse.qnt/types.qnt/alphabet.qnt/invariants.qnt/match.qnt/COVERAGE.md/ADR-0013 plus the live `dpaa2_api::REFUSAL_VARIANTS`/`ALL_FAMILIES`, and `ledger.rs:2250-2270` repeats the R14 live tie in the unit suite; negative tests mutate fixtures in both directions for R11 (rename + delete + phantom witness), R12 (rename ×2 + delete), R13 (orphan + ghost), R14, R15. Post-6.x names (`renameSelfNeutralizes`, `swapCorrect`, etc.) are enforced by the live legs, so the fixtures' synthetic names are fine. Confirmed PASS2's reading: R11 bijects refusal witnesses only (`parse_refusal_witnesses` skips `hasWarning`/structure shapes by construction, ledger.rs:929-960); the 9-variant `RawRefusal` vocabulary (raw_itf.rs:137) appears **nowhere** in ADR-0013 and has no lint — already PASS2's catalog row, not re-scored. Two stale doc lines inside the lint bodies: F4, F7.

**adapter.rs retro-model leg vs the reshaped api:** healthy. The 6.6-era retrofit onto `dpaa2_api::Family` (adapter.rs:34-90) is R14-tied and keeps only D10-mandated serde/ITF plumbing local; `replay.rs:183-192` correctly drops `SetLabel` from the conformance diff with the ADR-0015-decision-9 rationale, and `RETRO_PORT_NAME` keeps `Create` labels matched on both sides. One miscitation (F5) and one shared-scan triplication (F2). Board files (snapshot/safety/ioctlpolicy/mcstatus/driver/generate) carry zero intent vocabulary (content-grep; no Bash available for `git log`) — no cross-vintage staleness leaked in.

## Mandate verdicts

**(a) Sans-io:** shim is thin plumbing except the unsized-queues substitution (F2, a recorded contract, flagged as the collision point). `parse.rs` maps shape only. `engine.rs` is dispatch/verdict-plumbing; the verdict is core-side (one loudness gap, F4).

**(b) Refusal typing:** `QueueEnvelopeExceeded` stays a discriminated payload-carrying variant from `refuse/tenant.rs` through the ADR-0014-linted name list to render and ITF; no string-matching found anywhere on the refusal path. Dead-option refusals fold to `Error::Config` only as the terminal render (`From`, one-way, the recorded crate idiom), never re-parsed. The one typed-error hole is F3: `stamp_label` on the create chain bypasses the typed-classification funnel.

**(c) Reuse-before-write:** the +453 reuses `run_verb` (single classified exit), `create_and_plug`, the new generic `provision_chain` (explicitly written as the reusable transactional primitive), and `parse.rs` seams; encode/decode share one `const fn dpni_opt_bit` table; the dpni observation follows the dprc precedent exactly (separate observed type, honest-gap `Option`, raw struct keeps informational fields). No duplication findings; the shim-side wire-bit table is ADR-0018-deliberate.

**(d) Seams for #6/#7/#9/#10:** `families::dpni` pre-shapes #10 as D1 promises — `RuntimeState` grows fields without reshaping `Dpni`, `DpniObservation` is a separate type a portal read-back maps into adapter-side, and `drift_disposition`'s destroy+create arm is backend-agnostic (LAW 1, not a restool artifact). The shipped immutability is encapsulation + `compile_fail` doctest, not the literal "type parameter" tasks.md promised (F7) — and it is the better shape. The one create-only assumption a portal backend must confront is the shim-local dependency chain inside `create_dpni` (F8). `observe_container(id)` is dprc-scoped and family-generic (residents keyed by `ObjectRef`, no dpni hardcoding) — it generalizes. **D6 disposition:** the zero-value `Default` hazard is closed by construct (no `Default` on `Isolation`/`Intent`/`TenantRef`; the TOML default is solely the parser's rule; a zero-initialized kernel link-end is unrepresentable); the empty-`TenantName` hazard is closed at the sentinel mint by construct (macro `@sentinel` arm excludes it) with the ripple complete (Pass 1's grep stands), but not closed at the raw `From<&str>` boundary — one public call still constructs `""`, guarded by convention (F5). `from_name` keeping `""` as `Named` means the kernel-spoofing half of the hazard is structurally gone either way.

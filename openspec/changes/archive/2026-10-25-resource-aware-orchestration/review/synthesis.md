## 6. Rule amendment — nominated

The admission-basis defect (full-plan pricing refusing its own fixpoint) shipped through a sealed group with spec-align ACCEPT and was caught only by a user-driven semantics review; ledger #2 is the identical blind-spot class on the model side (replay never re-derives the verdict it replays). One existing-rule failure, twice, same shape. Nominate: **every judgment the engine renders ships with a self-application check at acceptance — (a) a fixpoint test (the judgment applied to its own converged output yields the identity verdict) and (b) a twin-side re-derivation assertion in replay (the model trace's verdict re-derived from the prior state through the Rust judgment).** Bead A's check_pair assertion is (b)'s first instance; `converged_intent_re_admits_against_zero_free` is (a)'s template.

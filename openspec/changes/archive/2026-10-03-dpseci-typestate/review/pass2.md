# PASS 2: dpseci-typestate — Isomorphism and the census law

HEAD unchanged from brief (ae23721 grounding stands). Verdict up front: the P2 family is structurally isomorphic law-for-law, guard-for-guard, refusal-for-refusal; the census is lawful two-sided with the observable subset exactly `(num_queues, options)` and no identity minted anywhere; ITF replay covers the whole create/refusal surface. Five findings — none breaks the census or the family twin; the two sharpest are a D9 role-doc contradiction on `classify_cfg_mismatch` and a D4 observe-path attribution claim the decode does not honor.

## Mandate verdicts

**(a) P2 isomorphism — HOLDS.** `dpseci.qnt` `dpseci_lifecycle` ↔ `families/dpseci.rs`: `DpseciOpt`/`MC_VOCABULARY` (3 flags, closed, ADR-0014 parity tests), `RawEscape`/`RawBits`, `OptionMask` (flags+escapes, additive-only builders — no path admits an unnamed flag), `Refusal` (3 variants, names verbatim), `classify` order transcribed into `DpseciCfg::new` (queue envelope → count match → per-entry range) and bound both directions by the proptest twin (`dpseci_replay.rs:242-269`, straddling all three bounds). DPSECI-I1 by construction on both sides: the model has no mutating verb; Rust has private fields, no setter, the `compile_fail` doctest (families/dpseci.rs:256-263), and `McControl` carries only create/destroy/observe dpseci verbs (contract/mc.rs:426-455). I4 is a pure function of create options on both sides, tested both polarities. On the brief's "refined newtypes" phrasing: the length-coupling is a *relational* constraint, so the shipped block-level refinement (`DpseciCfg` smart constructor; a violating block cannot exist post-construction) is the only structural encoding — per-field `Priority` newtypes would move the refusal out of restool's parse order and break the classify twin. Judged satisfied in substance; no finding.

**(b) Census lawful two-sided — HOLDS.** `sigCensus/censusDelta/censusConverged/observableSigOf/classifyObservableMismatch` ↔ `sig_census/census_delta/census_converged/sig_of/classify_observable_mismatch` operator-for-operator (plan/dpseci.rs:40-170); count-map encoding faithful; priorities excluded by `sig_of` and `DpseciDetail.tx_priorities` never judged (display only). No per-object identity on the path: `judge_dpseci` (dpaa2-mc/src/populate.rs:140-166) filters by custody label, carries `ObjectRef` only as the destroy handle, matching is pure multiset; position-independence tested at model (`censusPositionIndependentTest`), core (plan/dpseci.rs:331), and adapter (`dpseci_reordering_crypto_blocks_is_position_independent`, populate.rs:1066+). Two-blocks-per-tenant converges at THREE layers: `censusMatchEmptyDeltaTest`/`censusTwoSignaturesTest` (model), `census_match_empty_delta`/`census_two_signatures` (core), `dpseci_absent_creates_one_per_signature_and_is_idempotent` + `dpseci_matching_board_is_zero_actions` (adapter, FakeBackend). Role separation holds in shipped behavior — but only vacuously for `classify_cfg_mismatch` (F1/F2 below).

**(c) ITF replay coverage — create surface COMPLETE, census surface hand-mirrored.** All 8 frozen traces map: 3 refusal arms (each with its named variant asserted at the final state, `refusal_traces_create_nothing_and_name_the_class`), accept+verbatim read-back, raw-escape attribution, I4 both polarities. The property twin binds length-coupling and both range judgments bidirectionally. Arms with NO replayed trace: the `destroy` action (dpseci.qnt:220-223 — no frozen trace and no directed run drives it; simulator-only) and the 7 census `run` tests (dpseci.qnt:489-569 — bound by hand-written twin tests, not ITF). F5.

**(d) Typed, not stringly — HOLDS with one attribution gap.** Unobservability is a sum end to end: `SigUnobservable` ↔ `ObservedSig::Unobservable` ↔ `DpseciPortalReadout::Unobservable` ↔ `ChildDpseci::Unobservable`; absence of evidence never reaches the census as drift (`judge_dpseci` short-circuits; `dpseci_unobservable_portal_judges_nothing` proves zero actions and the seeded object survives). `reason` strings are display payloads only; the sole `.contains` assertions on them (restool.rs:2776,2780) are the recorded lbk.14 open-context deviation's pin test — protected, not a finding. No sentinel anywhere. The gap: the *read-side* decode never mints a `RawEscape` (F4).

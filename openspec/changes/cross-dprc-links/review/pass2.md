# Pass 2: Isomorphism and the connection-surface law (cross-dprc-links)

Repo root: . Verdict summary per mandate, then the findings table.

**(a) Isomorphism (ADR-0002):** HOLDS with drift at the edges. The edge-kind table mirrors `core/connect.qnt` rather than re-deriving it — `LEGAL_PAIRS` (connect.rs:28-36) is the seven unordered pairs of `legalPair` verbatim, `legal_ports` mirrors `legalPorts`, `edge_demands_severed_witness` mirrors its quint twin, and the proptest twin (link_replay.rs:515-523) pins symmetry over the full family space. The four wire phases, `ContainerBind`, `WireResidue`, `DeferredVisibility` names converge on the model's spelling; the Consent→Class-gate fold is explicitly recorded (connect.rs:582-584). The post-7.5 destroy-of-connected-end divergence (model ENABLED, Rust refuses as typestate POLICY) is recorded on the model side (link_lifecycle.qnt:234-235, 362-373) and protected by the brief — not a finding. The real drift is the LINK_I1 naming residue Pass 1 filed (F3/F4/F5, confirmed and extended below, including one site where connect.rs cites a model guard that no longer exists) and two unrecorded guard asymmetries (PASS2-F2, PASS2-F3).

**(b) Obligations typed, not checked:** MOSTLY HOLDS. `DeferredVisibility` has a private field and mints only inside `PostBindCreate::new` (pub(crate)), driven solely by `Container<Plugged>::create_resident_deferred` — unconstructible without its planned discharge. Declined consent is a typed residue that speaks its own name (`WireResidue::DeclinedVisibility` Display, connect.rs:566-571; `ChildDeferredVisibility` Display, :630-638). The stale node blocks nothing (nothing on any surface gates on it). Consent rides the existing ADR-0015 Class allow — engine.rs:1061-1074 judges through pure `discharge_child(obligation, cfg.allow)`, no parallel consent path, no Rust `Consent` enum. ONE leak: `PostBindCreate.discharge` is a public field, so the `RebindCycle` is reachable without the `discharge()` consent gate — the "no silent rebind representable" claim is type-law on the child twin but only engine discipline on the dpni twin (PASS2-F1).

**(c) ITF replay coverage:** 13/13 traces map onto model transitions (connect: wireLifecycle/crossContainer/childToChild/freshPartialOrder/bindBeforeConnect; disconnect+reconnect: wireLifecycle/reconnectAfterDisconnect; destroy: wireLifecycle/destroyConnectedEndRemovesEdge/postBindDestroyStaleNode; bind: freshPartialOrder/bindBeforeConnect; post-bind create: postBindCreateObligated; discharge both arms: consentedRebindDischarges/declinedConsentResidue; pair legality: dpniPairLegal; refusal: connectAlreadyConnectedRefused). The 27b99e2 re-freeze is clean: no trace or replay of the retired refusal; `destroyConnectedEndRemovesEdgeTest` replaces it and `accepted_destroy_removes_the_edge` (link_replay.rs:417-448) pins BOTH halves correctly — edge dies with the endpoint AND the POLICY refusal of the bare connect→destroy. Gaps: several guard/refusal arms have no replayed trace, and the refusal-witness claim in link_itf.rs is stronger than what the replayer does (PASS2-F5).

**(d) Bounded lift:** HELD. No dpmac code moved or re-keyed — `SeveredProof` (plan/transition.rs:59-68) untouched but for a doc cross-reference; no severed-witness logic duplicated for dpni↔dpni (`WireDisconnected` is a distinct disconnect-order proof, deliberately `Clone`, documented as unlike `SeveredProof`, connect.rs:204-206); the dpni↔dpni row is `DisconnectOnly` and `WireTransition` has no handback variant, so no handback path is representable. Pass 1's byte-unchanged dpmac replay seals it. The only wrinkle is that the table's "claiming" verbs overstate in prose (PASS2-F4).

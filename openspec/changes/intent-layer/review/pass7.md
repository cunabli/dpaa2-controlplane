## Answers to the questions handed to this pass

**Pass 2 open question 2 (the two candidate accidental triplications).** Neither is an accidental *Rust* copy — both are necessary copies that are merely unlinted, which ADR-0014 resolves with a lint leg, not a fold. The Family-name worry dissolves on inspection of the Rust side: both suspected extra copies scan `ALL_FAMILIES` instead of restating it — `crates/dpaa2-mc/src/parse.rs:100-105` (`parse_family_num` finds by `as_str`) and `crates/dpaa2-config/src/parse.rs:586-591` (`parse_family` ditto). The only genuinely hand-maintained lowercase list is the *model's* `FAMILY_NAMES` (`models/intent/intent_raw.qnt:153-157`), which must exist (Quint cannot derive `"dprc"` from the constructor `Dprc`) but is tied to `Family::as_str` by nothing (R14 bijects only the capitalized `variant_name` set against `core/types.qnt`, `ledger.rs:1189-1237`). Finding F1. The Warning pair is worse than Pass 2 counted: three spellings, not two — `refuse.qnt:66`, `refuse.rs:328`, plus stringly tag literals in verify's decoder (`intent_itf.rs:545,552`) — and both R11 (skips `hasWarning`, by construction) and R14 (no Warning leg) miss it. Finding F2.

**Pass 3 open question 3 (REF_INVENTORY).** PASS3-F10's fold is endorsed as-is (one `#[cfg(any(test, feature="testkit"))]` helper). After the fold the vocabulary is one hand copy + the generated qnt copy — ADR-0014 satisfied with no new lint; generating the Rust fixture too would be machinery for zero drift surface. Finding F4.

**Pass 4 open question 2 (RawRefusal: ADR section + lint, or DEVIATION?).** **DEVIATION stands; no ADR-0013 section, no R-rule.** Rationale: ADR-0013 §5 governs a *public Rust vocabulary* (the `Refusal` enum, `REFUSAL_VARIANTS`, operator-facing). `RawRefusal` has no Rust enum by design — `parse.rs` refuses with strings at the trust boundary, and the design records that posture (`intent_raw.qnt:121-131` explains each variant's parse.rs anchor; the `:43-53` DEVIATION records the set-vs-short-circuit relation). An ADR section would mint a third prose copy of a model-internal type, which ADR-0014 would then oblige us to lint — net machinery growth guarding nothing public. Drift is already loud twice over: the anchor-disjointness unit test hard-codes the parse.rs spellings (`raw_itf.rs:678-763`) and `raw_conformance.rs` replays every near-miss `Kind` end-to-end (the gqf.39-hole check). The one missing piece is the revisit trigger in writing: if dpaa2-config ever grows a public typed parse-error enum (a TUI rendering structured errors would force it), the vocabulary graduates to ADR-0013 §5 + R14 then. Finding F3, one-sentence amend.
